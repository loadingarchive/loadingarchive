Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Loading Archive ("we", "us") operates www.loadingarchive.com. This page explains what information is processed when you visit the site, why, and what your rights are. The short version: we don't have user accounts, we don't ask for personal information, and the only personal data processing on this site comes from standard web infrastructure and advertising.

Information we do not collect

Loading Archive has no registration, no login, no newsletter and no comment system. We never ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal details, and we do not build user profiles ourselves.

Server logs and infrastructure

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like virtually all web infrastructure, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and requested URL) to deliver the site securely and to protect against abuse. This data is processed by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy; we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads, which is how the site is funded. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Center.
  • You can opt out of some other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Visitors in the European Economic Area and the UK are shown a consent message before any personalized advertising cookies are set.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are visited and how often. This helps us decide which months and games to improve coverage for. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes usage data under Google's privacy policy. We only ever look at this data in aggregate.

Local storage

The site stores a small number of preferences in your browser's local storage — for example your chosen currency (USD/EUR/GBP) and filter settings. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

Embedded content

Game pages can embed trailers hosted by YouTube or Valve (Steam). Playing an embedded video is equivalent to visiting that third party's site: they may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies (YouTube/Google, Valve). Trailers only load after you press play.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. Since we don't hold personal data about you ourselves, most of these rights are exercised directly with the third parties named above (Google, Cloudflare). If you believe we hold any personal data about you — for example because you e-mailed us — contact us and we will access, correct or delete it on request. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is a general-audience release calendar and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site processes data — for example by adding a new analytics or advertising provider — we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? E-mail us at loadingarchive@outlook.com or use the contact page.
