What to contact us about

  • Corrections: a wrong release date, price, platform list or a game we've misclassified as a port or remake. Please include a link to the official source if you have one.
  • Missing games: an announced title that isn't in the calendar yet. Tell us the name and platform(s) and we'll add it.
  • Developers & publishers: want your game's listing updated with a better trailer, corrected details or a confirmed date? Reach out from a studio e-mail address.
  • Content removal: if you hold rights to media shown on this site and want something taken down, we'll handle it promptly.
  • Press & business: anything else, same address.

We typically respond within one to two days. For more about how the site works and where the data comes from, see the about page.
