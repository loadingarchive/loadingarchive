The release calendar that tells you what's actually new

Loading Archive tracks every upcoming video game release for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S and Nintendo Switch, with one difference from every other calendar: we label re-releases honestly. A Port badge means the same game is simply arriving on a new platform; a Remake badge means it has been visually or technically rebuilt. Both always show the original release date, so a ten-year-old game can never masquerade as a new one. These classifications are made by hand, not by an algorithm.

Release dates and platform lists are cross-checked daily against multiple sources, and prices come straight from Steam in USD, EUR and GBP, refreshed every day, including live discounts. The trending page shows what people are actually playing right now, updated hourly from Steam player counts.
